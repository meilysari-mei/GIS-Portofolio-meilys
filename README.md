🎓 Thesis / Undergraduate Research
Interactive Storybook — SPORE
Driven by admiration for the human brain's ability to process spatial information, this undergraduate research explores how to introduce spatial thinking to children through an interactive storybook. The result is SPORE, a storybook designed to scaffold early spatial ability in a fun and accessible way.
