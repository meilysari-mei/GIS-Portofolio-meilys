Undergraduate Research
Thesis Projects

During my undergraduate studies, my interest in GIS grew, particularly driven by my admiration for the human brain's ability to process spatial information. The question then arose: "How can we introduce or scaffold this ability at a very basic level?" This prompted me to research and develop a storybook for children that could introduce them to spatial abilities. The term "Spatial" is very close to us, but not many people realize it because they don't recognize it. Furthermore, to understand spatial information, we must be intimately familiar with what "spatial" is.
